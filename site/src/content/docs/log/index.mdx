One subject, told in pieces: **opening the Control4 controller line.** Each entry
below stands on its own. A single problem, how I went at it, and what it turned
out to actually be. Read them in any order. The
[reference sections](/hardware/matrix/) hold the settled facts; these hold
the reasoning.

Fair warning about the shape of it. Roughly half of these are about being wrong. A
fuse measurement at the wrong register offset held for weeks. A microcontroller
looked like dead hardware for days because the host was talking at the wrong
speed. Eight relays did nothing when driven correctly, because the pins were still
muxed to a camera port that doesn't exist on the board. None of that's tidied
away, because the wrong turns are most of what anyone can actually learn from.
